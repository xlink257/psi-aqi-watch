        How it works

        A scheduled task collects the readings every hour and stores them with this page. Country rankings rotate, so each country is refreshed every few hours. PSI figures for places outside Singapore are converted from US AQI using PM2.5 breakpoints, so they are marked ≈. Station positions in Singapore are approximate.
